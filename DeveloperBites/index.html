// about the project

A LITTLE CODE. A LOT OF FLAVOUR.

Bite Developer started with a simple idea: why should creativity stop at the screen?

As a software developer, I spend my days building things with code. But outside the world of programming, I enjoy experimenting with food, flavours and ideas.

That's how Bite Developer was born — a place where technology meets taste.

We believe good food doesn't need to be expensive or complicated. It just needs to be made with care, served fresh and enjoyed together.

Today, we're starting with a local favourite: Indori Garadu. Tomorrow, who knows what we'll develop next?
